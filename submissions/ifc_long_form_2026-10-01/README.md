# IFC long form: submission package of 1 October 2026

The two papers as sent for IFC consideration, typeset from the LaTeX sources
of that date, with editable Word versions. Text, equations and tables can be
edited in the Word files; diagrams and algorithm boxes are pictures there and
are changed in the LaTeX.

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `long_form.pdf`, `long_form.docx` | Governing privacy risk across repeated statistical releases: probability models, programme tolerances and accountable decisions |
| `online_appendix.pdf`, `online_appendix.docx` | Online appendix: case studies, the neural proposal method and SENTRY-R |

The LaTeX sources of this package were a snapshot of `paper/` as of 1 October
2026. They are not duplicated here: `paper/bis_ifc_full.tex` and
`paper/bis_ifc_online_appendix.tex` are the live sources and have moved on
since (the language-model arm, the ledger text). Rebuild from `paper/` for a
newer package; `.github/workflows/latex.yml` does so on every push.
